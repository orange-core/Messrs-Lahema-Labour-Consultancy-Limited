The Practice

01 MLC / NDOLA

Supporting clearer decisions in employment matters.

Workplace matters often involve more than a single question. They can affect employees, employers, contracts, procedures, relationships and the future direction of an organisation.

Messrs Lahema Labour Consultancy Limited approaches these matters by first understanding the circumstances, identifying the relevant employment issues and helping clients establish a practical way forward.

The practice covers a broad range of labour and employment matters, including contracts, workplace policies, disciplinary processes, disputes, employee rights, labour relations, compliance and employment termination.

The objective is straightforward: provide clients with a clearer understanding of the matter they are facing and the options available to them.
