WORKPLACE FOCUS

Supporting clearer decisions in employment matters.

Labour and employment issues rarely exist in isolation. A workplace dispute may involve a contract, a disciplinary process, an employment decision or a question of compliance at the same time.

Lahema's practice is structured around these connected workplace issues, with services covering both preventative and responsive employment matters.
