Ndola · Zambia
01 Scope of Practice

One workplace can involve many connected issues.

Employment matters often overlap. A disciplinary concern can involve procedure and workplace policy. A termination can involve contracts, employee rights and dispute resolution. Our service areas are therefore organised around the different stages and concerns that can arise in a working relationship.
